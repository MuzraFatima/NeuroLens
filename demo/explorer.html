2 · The Measurement & Method Gap SOURCE · MAIN_BENCHMARK_RESULTS.CSV

Single-Parent Letter Metrics Hide Multi-Parent Feature Absorption.

Real concepts inherit from multiple simultaneous parents: "bat" is both a mammal and a flying_creature starting with Letter 'B'; "Paris" is a capital_city, a noun.location, and TitleCase. Tree-structured SAEs allow at most one parent per child—so they score well on single-parent letters while silently absorbing the second and third parents.

BEFORE · TREE-PRIOR SAE (H-SAE, m ≤ 1)

Routes "bat" to Letter 'B' (1st-Letter Absorption: 1.25%), but drops mammal and flying_creature, leaving 28.29% Mean MPAbs and 18.69% Joint Absorption.

AFTER · DR-SAE (FREQUENCY-ORDERED DAG, m ≤ 8)

Routes child activations across all learned ancestors simultaneously via z_eff = (I − A)⁻¹ z, cutting Mean MPAbs to 3.99% (native L0) and 6.71% (matched effective L0 = 50.0).

3 · End-to-End Architecture & Mathematical Guarantees THEORY · LEMMA 1 & PROPOSITIONS 1–2

Four Differentiable Steps, Guaranteed Acyclic by Construction.

STEP 01 · ENCODE

BatchTopK Native Codes

Produces sparse native activations z = BatchTopK_k(W_enc(x − b_dec) + b_enc) at fixed average sparsity k = 50.

STEP 02 · ORDER (LEMMA 1)

Strict Frequency Rank

True parents fire strictly more often than children (f_p = τ f_c + (1−f_c)β > f_c). Masking A[p,c] to f_p > f_c guarantees a nilpotent DAG with zero cycles.

STEP 03 · ROUTE (PROP. 1)

Sparsity-Neutral Ancestry

Unrolls z_eff = Σ_{h=0}^H A^h z (H = 4). Every ancestor fires on child tokens at zero extra native L0 cost.

STEP 04 · ORTHO ATOMS

Whitened Residual Dictionary

Trains x̂ = R z_eff + b_dec with 4-bank Matryoshka loss, L1 edge pruning λ_A ‖A‖₁, and whitened orthogonality ⟨r̃_p, r̃_c⟩².

6 · Explicit Limitations & Uncertainty Bounds SCIENTIFIC HONESTY GUARD

What This Evidence Proves—and Where Its Boundaries Lie.

1. EFFECTIVE L0 TRADE-OFF

At native L0 = 50.0, ancestral routing raises effective L0 to 63.8. That is why our primary headline claim uses DR-SAE (Matched Eff. L0 = 50.0) (k = 38.2, MPAbs 6.71%, ΔFVU = +0.0036 ≤ 0.01).

2. THREE-SEED MDE FLOOR

With 3 random seeds (42, 137, 2026), cross-seed standard deviation is 0.45% MPAbs (Minimum Detectable Effect 4.2% relative). Differences smaller than 1 SD (such as H=4 vs H=8 at 3.99% vs 3.89%) are statistically indistinguishable.

3. LEMMA 1 NEAR-DUPLICATE BOUND

Under partial coverage τ < 1, frequency order drops an edge when β ≤ (1−τ)f_c / (1−f_c)—i.e., when parent p almost never fires without child c, which aligns with AG-SAE's non-redundancy criterion.
